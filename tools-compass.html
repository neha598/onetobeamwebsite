Why use this Compass?

PropCompass is a free tool for real estate agents and property consultants to check property orientation on-site with 100% accuracy.

As a real estate professional, knowing the exact direction (North, South, East, West) is crucial for checking property orientation and answering buyer questions on the spot.

This digital compass compensates for True North vs Magnetic North using device sensors, ensuring reliable readings directly inside your smartphone browser.

  • Identify Main Door Facing: Check orientation in seconds during client walkthroughs.
  • Vastu Shastra Insights: Live recommendations for entrances, kitchens, and master bedrooms.
  • Zero App Downloads: Instant access on any iOS Safari or Android Chrome browser.
  • Calibrated Precision: Smooth degree dampening with True North declination support.
