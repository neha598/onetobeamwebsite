From Floor Plan to Virtual Showflat: How It Works

A floor plan tells you how a property is designed. It shows the rooms, dimensions, entrances, balconies and overall layout. But for many buyers, understanding a home from a 2D drawing alone can be difficult.

By OneToBeam Editorial • October 2026 • 5 min read

This is where the process of going from floor plan to virtual tour becomes useful. By combining architectural information with 3D visualization, a simple floor plan can become an interactive experience that allows buyers to explore a property before it is built.

What Happens to the Floor Plan?

The process starts with the information already available from the developer or architect.

  • This can include:
  • 2D floor plans
  • CAD drawings
  • 3D models
  • Architectural renders
  • Material references
  • Furniture layouts
  • Apartment configurations

The floor plan provides the basic structure. The other information helps build the visual environment around it.

From 2D Layout to 3D Space

The next step is converting the floor plan into a three-dimensional representation.

Walls, rooms, doors, windows, balconies and other architectural elements are modelled based on the provided drawings. Furniture, materials and finishes can then be added to make the space easier to understand visually.

This is the key transition in a floor plan to virtual tour workflow: a flat architectural drawing becomes a space that can be viewed and explored.

  • Making the Virtual Showflat Interactive

Once the 3D environment is created, it can be turned into an interactive virtual showflat.

Instead of simply watching a video, buyers can move through different parts of the apartment, look around rooms, understand the layout and explore different areas at their own pace.

For example, a buyer could move from the living room to the balcony, look towards the bedroom and understand how the spaces connect.

The experience can also include different configurations, materials or design options where required.

Connect the Floor Plan With the Experience

The real value comes when the floor plan and virtual tour are connected.

A buyer could select an apartment from the project layout, open its floor plan and then enter its virtual representation. This creates a much clearer connection between what the drawing shows and what the space could look like.

For developers, this can be particularly useful for projects that are still under construction.

How i-PLEX Brings It Together

i-PLEX can take the floor plan to virtual tour concept further by connecting apartment visualization with the larger project experience.

  • A buyer can explore:

Master Layout → Building → Floor → Unit → Floor Plan → Configuration → Virtual Experience

This allows the apartment to be understood in context rather than as an isolated floor plan or standalone render.

Existing project content such as floor plans, renders, videos and 3D models can be brought together into one interactive platform.

Why Go From Floor Plan to Virtual Tour?

A floor plan is useful for understanding the technical layout. A virtual tour adds another layer by helping buyers visualize the space.

  • Together, they can help buyers:

Understand room connections and proportions

Explore the apartment remotely

  • Compare configurations

Visualize spaces before construction is complete

Get a better understanding of the proposed design

The goal isn't to replace the floor plan. It is to make the information behind it easier to experience.

From a drawing on a page to a space buyers can explore — that's the value of going from floor plan to virtual tour.
