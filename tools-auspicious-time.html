Why use Auspicious Time?

PropTime is a free tool for real estate agents and property consultants to instantly identify the most favorable time slots for deal closings and site visits.

Closing a deal or inaugurating a new home are major life events. Clients often want to ensure these happen at the most fortuitous moments.

Auspicious Time helps you instantly advise clients on the best timings ("Shubh Muhurat") and avoid inauspicious periods like Rahu Kalam.

  • Avoid Rahu Kalam: Automatically calculated for your exact location.
  • Plan Closings: Find the perfect slot for signing agreements.
  • Guide Clients: Position yourself as a thoughtful, culturally aware agent.
  • Instant & Free: No need to consult an almanac manually.
