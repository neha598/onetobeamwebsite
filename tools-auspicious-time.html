Why use Auspicious Time?

Auspicious Time is a free tool for real estate agents and property consultants to instantly identify the most favorable time slots for deal closings and site visits.

Closing a deal or inaugurating a new home are major life milestones. Clients often want to ensure these happen at fortuitous moments.

Auspicious Time calculates real-time astronomical Muhurats based on your exact coordinates, so you can advise clients with confidence.

  • Avoid Rahu Kalam: Automatically calculated for your exact GPS coordinates.
  • Plan Closings: Find the ideal slot for signing sale deeds and agreements.
  • Guide Clients: Position yourself as a thoughtful, culturally aware consultant.
  • 100% Free: Instant calculations without consulting almanacs manually.
