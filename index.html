Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About
i-PLEX.

What is i-PLEX?

i-PLEX is an interactive virtual sales gallery for property developments. It connects masterplans, buildings, floor plans, 3D/360° renders, amenities, and project details into a single seamless digital experience for buyers and sales teams.

What is a virtual sales gallery?

A virtual sales gallery is a digital platform that brings the complete sales gallery experience online. It allows buyers and sales representatives to explore an entire development—including site layouts, unit configurations, and views—from anywhere in the world.

Can I use my existing CGI and 360° renders?

Yes. Through our BYOC (Bring Your Own Content) model, i-PLEX can integrate your existing CGI renders, 360° panoramas, floor plans, drone footage, site plans, and videos created by your current architects or visualization studios.

What does BYOC mean?

BYOC stands for "Bring Your Own Content." If you already have marketing assets from your existing creative partners or agencies, you can provide them directly. We structure and power your content within the interactive i-PLEX platform without requiring you to recreate anything.

Do I need a 3D model to use i-PLEX?

No, a 3D model is not strictly required. i-PLEX can be built using 2D floor plans, standard CGI renders, 360° panoramas, masterplan drawings, and photography. If you have 3D models, they can also be incorporated.

Can OneToBeam create the content for us?

Yes. Through i-PLEX Studio, OneToBeam provides end-to-end visualization services. We can create 3D renders, CGI, 360° virtual tours, floor plans, and animations from scratch, and deliver the completed interactive sales experience.

Can i-PLEX be embedded into our existing website?

Yes. i-PLEX is web-native and can be embedded directly into your existing property development or corporate website using standard embed code or an iframe.

Does i-PLEX work on mobile and tablet devices?

Yes. i-PLEX runs entirely in modern web browsers and is fully responsive across desktop computers, tablets, and smartphones without requiring any app download or installation.

Can i-PLEX be used in a physical sales gallery?

Yes. The exact same i-PLEX experience can power large-format touchscreens, interactive kiosks, digital video walls, and sales tablets inside your on-site sales gallery or roadshow presentation.

Can property marketing agencies use or white-label i-PLEX?

Yes. We partner with property marketing agencies, branding agencies, and visualization studios through white-label and production-partner models, allowing you to offer interactive sales technology directly to your developer clients under your brand.

Where is project content hosted?

i-PLEX is a cloud-hosted platform. All project assets, interactive tours, and media are hosted on secure, high-performance cloud infrastructure for fast global loading, with nothing required to be installed or maintained locally.

Can i-PLEX be used for projects that are not yet constructed?

Yes, this is one of its primary use cases. i-PLEX is built specifically for off-plan property sales, helping buyers visualize and understand masterplans, towers, floor layouts, and unit finishes long before construction is completed.
